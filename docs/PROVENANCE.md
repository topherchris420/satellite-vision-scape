# Evidence and provenance

Evidence classes are `OBSERVED`, `REPORTED`, `RECONSTRUCTED`, `DERIVED`, `LIVE`, `HISTORICAL`, `ESTIMATED`, `SIMULATED`, and `ILLUSTRATIVE`. They are not mutually interchangeable. In particular, freshness does not turn reported data into direct observation, and attractive rendering does not turn reconstruction into fact.

Every provenance record identifies source/URL, license, retrieval method, transform chain, attribution, temporal fields, uncertainty, and limitations. The viewer exposes a compact provenance panel and manifest detail. Derived terrain results retain the full source manifest, algorithm inputs/version, units, and limitation statement.

The platform is for defensive public-source awareness and reproducible analysis. It must not express unsupported certainty or facilitate targeting, covert collection, private surveillance, or identity tracking.
