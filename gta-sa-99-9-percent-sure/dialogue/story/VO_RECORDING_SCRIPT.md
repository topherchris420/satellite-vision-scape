- **SENTINEL**: SENTINEL, CERTAIN's successor. Even smoother. Even surer.
- **DISPATCH**: Police radio dispatcher. Flat, procedural, radio-filtered.

- **NEWS**: TV news anchor. Measured broadcast cadence.
- **REX**: Rex Pattern, late-night talk host. Gravel, conviction, every sentence a revelation.
- **REXLIVE**: Rex Pattern in person. Same voice, less reverb.
- **CALLER**: Late-night radio caller. Sincere, a bit spooked.
- **TECH**: SureThing field technician. Tired, underpaid, not a villain.

- **COURIER**: Bike courier, Downtown. Talks fast, rides faster.

- **TEACHER**: Garcia high-school physics teacher. Thrilled to get real equipment.
