# Does removing semantic bearings make Jev's navigation worse?

<sub>Experiment `semantic-bearings-navigation` · generated by `bun run experiment report semantic-bearings-navigation` from the trace files below · complete</sub>

## Question

Jev is told directions in words ("ahead and to the left"). Does it navigate the coffee run worse when told signed degrees instead?

## Hypothesis

Removing semantic_bearings (and nothing else) makes the coffee run worse for Jev: at least 10 s slower to deliver, through more wrong or wasted choices.

**We would reconsider it if:**

- no-bearings is no more than 10 s slower (median paired change): the assist does not earn its bytes on this run, and its probes decide retirement

**It is supported if:**

- no-bearings takes at least 10 s longer to deliver in a clear majority of paired runs

## Why we asked

semantic_bearings was added on TypeSafe's integration guidance, not after an observed failure. It is the one assist with no failure behind it, so it is the cheapest to test and the likeliest to retire. Navigation itself is done by the executor; Jev only picks the destination, so the effect may be small.

- src/server/agent/assists.ts: semantic_bearings, evidence 'TypeSafe integration guidance'
- docs/NEXT_MODEL.md §1: probes coffee-deliver-while-driving, terminal-go-to-terminal

## Design

- **Independent variable:** `assists`: What Jev's question includes: a profile (`full`, `lean`, `none`), a single-factor ablation (`full-<assist>`) or a list of assist ids.
- **Held fixed:** `provider` = `jev`
- **Episode:** from the start of After Hours until the coffee reaches the technician (at most 300 simulated s)
- **Runs:** 5 per condition; run *i* of every condition uses seed 1 + *i* (paired)

| Condition | Setting | Provider | Runs | Repeats exactly? |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | --: | :-- |
| `full` Every assist (run B's question) | `assists` = `full` | jev | 5 / 5 | no (seeded or live) |
| `no-bearings` Every assist but semantic_bearings | `assists` = `full-semantic_bearings` | jev | 5 / 5 | no (seeded or live) |

## Limitations

- Live: Jev's latency and choices vary between runs, and pairing by seed only aligns the order of runs, not the model's randomness.
- Five runs per condition can show a large effect; they cannot rule out a small one.
- The coffee run exercises two navigation choices; the terminal stage (four destinations) is covered by the probes.
- Metrics are computed in the client from the session's own records; nothing here is server-attested.
